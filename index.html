About Arnie Rose

Arnie Rose is the author of the You Were Never Series, a collection of books exploring identity, behavior, and the stories people carry about themselves.

Her work focuses on self-leadership, decision-making, and personal growth.

She also writes about project management, supply chain management, and operational thinking.
