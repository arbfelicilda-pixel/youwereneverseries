Not Everyone Blooms in the Same Season

Some flowers bloom in spring. Others bloom in summer. Some only open fully after difficult weather has passed. Some take years before they show what they are capable of.

Yet no flower is considered wrong for blooming later than another. No garden judges a rose for not being a daffodil.

People are not all on the same seasonal schedule. Some find their direction early. Some spend decades searching before something clicks. Some need the hard years before they become capable of the good ones.

If you are in a season that does not feel like blooming — a season of difficulty, confusion, or quiet work — that does not mean your season is not coming. It means it has not arrived yet.

Your season will arrive. And when it does, you will realize that the waiting was not wasted time. It was the preparation that made it possible.
